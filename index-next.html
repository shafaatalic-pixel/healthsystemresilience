§ 04 · From argument to application
An HSREP initiative in development
44.89%

of eligible patients at the average U.S. community health center complete colorectal cancer screening. In Michigan, 48.74%. The recommendation is made; the screening is not completed. HSREP calls that the Prevention Adoption Gap.

Prevention Adoption Initiative

The first demonstration is deliberately small: one Southeast Michigan safety-net clinic, one screening measure, twelve months, and an independent academic evaluator. It has not begun. It needs a host site and an evaluator, and the design is public so both can judge it before a conversation.
