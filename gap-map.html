    06 · The next step · NCQA COF-E, 2027

    A screening test is step one. A positive test needs a colonoscopy.

    A screening rate counts people who completed a test. It cannot show how many positive stool tests never became a colonoscopy. From 2027, NCQA's new COF-E measure will count that step for adults 45 to 75, within 180 days.

    07 · How this is made

    Transparent by design.

    Checked against HRSA's file on November 6, 2026. Data year 2025; the next update is expected in mid 2027.

    Source and period

    HRSA Uniform Data System, Health Center Program awardee data, reporting year 2025 (file H80-2025, Table 6B, colorectal cancer screening, adults 46 to 75), refreshed by HRSA on May 7, 2026. Target: Healthy People 2030, objective C-07, 72.8%. HRSA data downloads · Healthy People 2030

    What we calculate

    Points below target = 72.8 minus the published rate. More adults to target = 72.8% of eligible adults minus those screened, rounded up, never below zero. Michigan figures add up all 39 centers' published totals (91,450 of 187,620). Urban and rural figures use only centers with a published rate. HRSA notes it cannot verify data others reprocess, so these sums are HSREP's.

    Rates HRSA did not publish

    Four centers have no published rate for 2025. They appear in grey. No rate, gap or count is estimated or shown for them, in any view or export.

    Sites and the map

    All 383 permanent clinic sites that Michigan's 39 health centers reported as active on December 31, 2025 (HRSA Form 5B), placed at the center of their ZIP code, so nearby sites can overlap. For 28 sites HRSA's file has no ZIP code; they are placed by the ZIP code of their street address (U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Seasonal sites (44) and mobile vans (29) are not shown. Every site shows its health center's rate.

    Names are shown in title case and everyday word order (HRSA lists "County of Ingham, Health Department"). Two spellings in HRSA's file are corrected: "Institutute" and "Catherines".

    Not a ranking

    A lower rate often reflects patients who move often, lack housing or a phone, speak other languages, or live far from colonoscopy, not weaker care. The list is ordered A to Z by default; the planning view orders by the number of adults, for partners deciding where help would reach the most people.

    Updates and citation

    Updated once a year when HRSA releases new data. Each yearly edition gets its own DOI. Cite as: HSREP. Prevention Gap Map, Michigan, data year 2025. hsraep.org/gap-map.
