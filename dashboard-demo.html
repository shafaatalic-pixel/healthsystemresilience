Built · free to try · seeking its first host clinic The Completion Worklist

Get the follow-up done, not just found.

A worklist for the step after a positive or due screening result. It tells staff who to contact first and why, a named person approves every message, approved messages go out and visits get booked through the clinic’s own accounts, and it shows what is working. Try every screen now, with invented people.

02 · What it does

Five jobs, one screen at a time.

Contact peopleA ranked list with the reason beside each code, a next step for every person, a step plan (text, call, letter, visit), caseloads and handover notes.
Check and send safelyDrafts from signed-off wording; a person ticks five checks; edited, translated or late messages need a second person; practice checks measure how carefully.
Handle repliesSTOP and every opt-out word, in four languages, stops all contact at once. A YES or a question waits for a person.
See what worksOn-time completion on closed windows, fairness across groups, a random comparison group with an honest range, and a quarterly report for funders.
Make it readyClinical values signed by your lead, a compliance pack for your officer, guides for your own export, and a checklist that keeps real lists locked until it is done.

05 · Built to be safe

What the tool will not do.

  • It never sends without a person. Every message is approved under a name, and the history shows if anything was changed afterwards.
  • It never takes names. Files with names, record numbers, full dates, phone numbers or emails are refused whole.
  • It never ignores a stop. Every opt-out word works at once, everywhere.
  • It never sends real US patient messages through a service that will not sign a HIPAA agreement. US texts follow the healthcare texting limits: one a day, three a week, 160 characters.
  • It never shows small groups outside the clinic. Groups under 11 are hidden, with extra groups hidden so they cannot be worked out.

07 · Questions clinics ask

Before you book.

Does any patient data leave our clinic?

No list is kept by HSREP. A list stays in the browser it is opened in. Messages leave only through your clinic’s own accounts, after a person approves them.

Does it work with our EHR?

It reads the export you already make. There are guides for Epic, eClinicalWorks, athenaOne, OCHIN, DHIS2 and Simple. A direct EHR connection comes after a pilot.

Can it text our patients?

Through a texting service that signs a HIPAA agreement with your clinic. Until that is in place, texts go out through your own system as a hand-off file.

Does it use AI?

In the demo, AI can draft messages from invented data. With real lists, AI drafting would run only under your clinic’s data agreement, and a person approves every word either way.

What does it cost?

The demo is free. For a pilot, the clinic pays for my time as implementation lead under a written agreement; HSREP adds no fee on top. See the pilot page for planning ranges.

08 · For your IT team

One coded file. The key stays with you.

For the colorectal pilot the worklist reads 19 coded columns from one nightly file, with dates sent as day counts. Other programmes use the same idea with their own columns; the demo has a guide for each export.

The exact file a site would send, 19 columns
patient_idSite-generated code. The key that links it to a patient stays at the site. No name, MRN, DOB, address or phone leaves your system.
birth_yearYear only: enough to apply the 45 to 75 window of the follow-up measure (COF-E, HEDIS MY2027).
sex, preferred_languageReporting and outreach-channel matching.
zip3First three digits only, per the HIPAA Safe Harbor rule.
payer_typeCoded category, not a policy number.
screening_statusoverdue | fit_negative | fit_positive | colonoscopy_completed
days_overdueDays since the patient became due. The first ranking rule.
days_since_last_fit, last_fit_resultDays since the most recent stool test, and its result. A day count, not a date.
days_since_positive_fitStarts the COF-E clock and is the second ranking rule. The single most important field. A day count, not a date.
colonoscopy_status, days_to_colonoscopyopen | scheduled | completed, and days from the positive test to the colonoscopy. Closes the cascade.
prior_no_shows_24moCount. The third ranking rule.
portal_active, preferred_contactWhich channel actually reaches this person.
sdoh_flagsPipe-separated: transport | language | food | housing | cost. Optional.
pcp_id, site_idPanel attribution for multi-provider or multi-site reporting.
